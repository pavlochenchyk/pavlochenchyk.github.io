Experiment 2

Sorting offers by repeat rate

Some writers kept customers much better than others, so we showed them first

The biggest drop after the first purchase was between the first and second order, so that was the largest untapped growth. We changed how offers are sorted: writers with a higher repeat rate, the share of customers who come back for a second order, moved up the list. First-time customers saw them first and were more likely to come back for the next order.

Why it worked

Interviews showed it wasn’t about writing better. Customers came back to writers who were easy to work with:

  1. 01Polite, clear communication
  2. 02Quick replies to messages
  3. 03Orders delivered on time
  4. 04Revisions done on time
  • Repeat orders+6.9%

Long-running A/B test, statistically significant

Experiment 3

A step-by-step order form

Many customers left the order form without filling in a single field

They saw every required field on one screen, decided it would take too long and put it off, often for good. My goal was to make the first step as easy as a single tap and draw people into filling in the form.

  1. 01One question at a time, so the form never looks long
  2. 02Popular options as one-click chips under every field
  3. 03Each answer collapses into a line with the chosen option, so customers see their progress and can change any answer
  • First orders+12.5%
  • Repeat orders+6.2%

Long-running A/B test, statistically significant
