Three A/B tests over 1.5 years, each on a different step of the funnel. Each started from a big drop-off in the data, checked against customer interviews

  1. Experiment 1 Offer card redesign Choosing a writer was the biggest drop-off before payment. Each order drew 30+ offers that all looked the same, so I redesigned the offer card around what customers look for. +14%chose a writer+6%first-order conversion+3.5%second-order conversion Read the case
  2. Experiment 2 Sorting by repeat rate Writers whose customers come back for a second order moved up the offer list. Interviews showed it was about communication and deadlines, not better writing. +6.9%second-order conversion Read the case
  3. Experiment 3 Step-by-step order form A long form with many required fields became one question at a time, with popular options as one-click chips. +12.5%first-order conversion+6.2%second-order conversion Read the case

Second-order conversion: from the first to the second order. Long-running A/B tests, all results statistically significant
