  1. Experiment 1 Offer card redesign Choosing a writer was the biggest drop-off before payment. Each order drew 30+ offers that all looked the same, so I redesigned the offer card around what customers look for. +14%chose a writer+6%first orders+3.5%repeat orders Read the case
  2. Experiment 2 Sorting by repeat rate Writers whose customers come back for a second order moved up the offer list. Interviews showed it was about communication and deadlines, not better writing. +6.9%second orders Read the case
