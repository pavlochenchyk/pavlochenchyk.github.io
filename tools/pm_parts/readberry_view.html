Acquisition

The challenge

Find at least one topic that works, an audience and an angle that convert profitably, by running and burning through topics and creatives on a limited budget. We launched in summer, the low season when much of the audience is passive, and chose not to wait for September. The whole ad budget for the unit economics test was about $52k, so the goal was to find a working combination, not to scale.

Finding the audience

From day one we tagged every buyer by archetype, based on their quiz answers. By August the data showed who we attracted most: nursing students and nurses. We moved the main focus to them and tested campaigns and creative topics for this audience. Blended CAC went from $263 in June to $68 in September.

Creatives that worked

A funnel built around the audience

In parallel we built a dedicated nursing funnel and a custom product page in the App Store, and optimised individual steps. The quiz moved in stages: easy warm-up questions first, then their professional experience, then the pain of falling behind and having no time for family, and only then the app’s features and personal setup.

About 1 in 4 visitors reached the profile questions; 6.8% of them bought within 7 days, up to 11% in the best segments.
