Context

  • iOS app: MVP built in 2 months
  • Marketing channels: Meta Ads via a web-to-app funnel, Apple Search Ads, ASO
  • English-speaking market, mostly the US

Acquisition

The challenge

Show that the app can be grown with paid ads at all: find audiences that bring a healthy CAC and LTV. We launched in the low season and chose not to wait for the peak. The whole ad budget for the test was about $52k, so the goal was to find working audiences, not to scale.

Blended CAC by stage

  1. LaunchSearching for working creatives and audiences on a small budget.
  2. SearchBurning through angles and audiences to find a combination that converts.
  3. Audience foundInsight: nurses. Tagging every customer by the materials they uploaded showed nursing most often, though we never targeted it.
  4. ScaleFull launch on the nursing audience, with a funnel and creatives built for it. Switched to intro offers.

Blended CAC: all ad spend on Meta and Apple Search Ads divided by new subscriptions.

A funnel built around the audience

  • A separate funnel and a custom App Store product page for nurses
  • 17 quiz screens before the paywall
  • Easy start: age, work, reading time, goals
  • People rate their own pain points: losing focus, rereading, phone distractions
  • The app is set up before paying: voice, speed, reading mode, highlights, quiz frequency
  • A personal plan right before the price
  • Core audience: 35–44 years old, 40% of quiz takers and the best conversion, 7.4%
  • ADHD and dyslexia answers converted best: up to 11% vs 6.8% on average

About 1 in 4 visitors started the quiz, and 86% of them reached the plans. 6.8% of everyone who answered bought.

What we shipped

  • InputKeep OriginalSmart Cleanup adapted text for TTS and filled in gaps between pages. After uploading, people listened less than we expected. We added a mode that keeps the original text and only removes clutter like headers and page numbers. Users split about evenly between the two.
  • PlayerNew player coreText highlighting fell out of sync with audio. After the rebuild, player stalls dropped from about 57 to under 1 per 100 listening sessions.
  • QuizzesQuizzes as a separate featurePeople finished their material and scrolled straight to the quizzes. We gave quizzes their own tab, so a finished text can be practiced right away. Quiz answers grew from about 800 to 2,600–3,500 a week.

What we planned next

  • Kindle integrationConnect a Kindle library and turn any book into audio, the way ElevenReader does.
  • Content from a promptGenerate study material from a prompt, so people don't have to find a document first.
  • Follow-up materialOffer a new generated text on the same topic once the current one is finished.
  • FlashcardsShipped, but we stopped before measuring the effect.

Outcome

The test answered its question: Readberry can grow on paid ads. CAC came down from $263 to $68, and LTV/CAC grew from 0.4× to 1.3×. The next question was whether scaling it would pay off.

Scale plans: cumulative result

Plan we presentedPeak investment −$1.9M · break-even month 20 · payback month 36
Leaner planSpend capped at $650k a month, paused in high-CAC months. Peak investment −$1.4M · break-even month 15 · payback month 29

Decision

Stop before scaling: the money at risk was too large for how slowly and how fragilely it came back.

  • The scale plan needed up to $1.9M and paid back only in month 36.
  • Even the leaner plan took until month 29, and 15% higher CAC pushed payback to month 39.
  • After store and payment fees, LTV/CAC was about 1.1×: each ad dollar barely came back within 13 months.
