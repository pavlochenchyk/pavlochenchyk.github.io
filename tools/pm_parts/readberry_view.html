Context

  • USP: not just a reader like Speechify or ElevenReader. Built-in quizzes break up monotonous listening, keep attention on the content and let you check yourself
  • Three audiences: students and professionals who have to read a lot, readers short on time, and people with ADHD or dyslexia
  • Hard paywall, no free trials ever
  • iOS app: MVP built in 2 months, on sale June – October 2026
  • Marketing channels: Meta Ads via a web-to-app funnel, Apple Search Ads, ASO
  • English-speaking market, mostly the US

Acquisition

The challenge

Show that the app can be grown with paid ads at all: find audiences that bring a healthy CAC and LTV. We launched in summer, the low season, and chose not to wait for September. The whole ad budget for the test was about $52k, so the goal was to find working audiences, not to scale.

A funnel built around the audience

  • A separate funnel and a custom App Store product page for nurses
  • 17 quiz screens before the paywall
  • Easy start: age, work, reading time, goals
  • People rate their own pain points: losing focus, rereading, phone distractions
  • The app is set up before paying: voice, speed, reading mode, highlights, quiz frequency
  • A personal plan right before the price

About 1 in 4 visitors started the quiz, and 86% of them reached the plans. 6.8% of everyone who answered bought.

Monetization

Monthly plan experiments on web

We were looking for a price that matches the pain and the budget of our users, and for the balance between the first and the second payment. A cheap entry kept people but did not pay back. The intro offer kept the low entry and made the renewal pay.
